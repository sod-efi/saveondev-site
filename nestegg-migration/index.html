The part of the migration nobody does for you

eMoney and RightCapital don't integrate. The plan data has an import path. The vault — years of statements, tax returns, estate documents, insurance policies — does not. That part is manual, unless you hand it to us.

What RightCapital's importer covers

  • Plan data — accounts and planning figures extracted from reports

What we cover

  • Every vault document — moved from the eMoney Vault to the matching RightCapital folder
  • Folder fidelity — your source structure is mapped, and new folders are created where nothing on the RightCapital side matches
  • Per-file verification — every transferred file is hash-checked, so "moved" means provably identical
  • A written record — a deterministic log of what moved, where it landed, and the short list of exceptions, for your compliance file

Questions firms ask us

Why not just use RightCapital's own migration help?

Use it — for plan data. Their Smart Import reads planning reports and cuts data-entry time. It does not move vault documents. The vault is the part that otherwise lands on your staff, one download and upload at a time.

What does it cost?

A fixed fee for the whole engagement, quoted in writing after the scoping call, based on client and document counts. The first client is migrated first so you can judge the work before the full fee is committed.

What if our folder structure doesn't match RightCapital's?

That's normal, and it's handled: matching folders are mapped, missing ones are created with sensible names, and every created folder and exception appears in your verification log.

Do you change anything in our eMoney account?

No. The source is read-only throughout. Files are verified at the destination against the source, and your eMoney vault remains exactly as it was — you retire it on your own schedule.

How long does a firm-wide migration take?

It depends on document volume, and the scoping call produces a schedule you can hold us to. The first client proves the pace before the rest of the book moves.

Who actually does the work?

The Nest Egg Guru team — the same people who built the migration tooling and ran the first migration on a live firm's most complex account. You're not handed off to a subcontractor.
